# 밤눈 product video: brief, style guide, beat sheet

**Film in one line:** a calm, 66-second Korean product film where Ollie, the
device's own voice, explains why night drivers doze and how Bamnun wakes
them, ending at our booth's QR code.

**For:** the 창보고 exhibition booth (tablet loop, so every line is also on
screen) and the final presentation. Korean first; small English subtitles.

## Style guide

- **Palette:** night `#141218`, panel `#1F1C24`, line `#2E2A33`, text
  `#F2EEE8`, muted `#9A93A0`, accent cream `#E8D7AE` (the case's logo
  colour). Colour only where it means something physical: the blue light
  `#4C7DFF`, the mint mist `#8FE3C8`, the alarm amber `#F2B66D`.
- **Type:** Pretendard (as the app). Headlines 84–120 px bold, Korean first;
  English below at ~40% size in muted grey. Subtitles: Korean 44 px, English
  26 px, bottom centre on a soft dark band.
- **Motion grammar:** slow and calm. Things ease in (cubic out, 0.5–0.8 s),
  rise 24 px while fading in, never bounce except Ollie. One idea per shot.
  Scene changes: a 0.5 s fade through night. Numbers count up. Lights glow
  on with a 0.3 s ramp.
- **Banned:** gradient backgrounds, neon glow on text, centred
  text-on-gradient AI look, stock icons, fake UI. Every product screen is a
  real capture (the live website) or our real dashboard style.
- **Sound:** Ollie's narration on top; a quiet, warm synth pad underneath
  (80 BPM); soft UI ticks on chips; the device's real chime and alarm on the
  stage beats.
