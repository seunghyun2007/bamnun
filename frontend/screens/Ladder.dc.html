How Ollie wakes you

Four steps, from a soft glow to an alarm. Each one only fires if the last one wasn't enough.

Blue light

A dim blue glow at the edge of your view, held 30 min.

Always

Mint mist

A 10 s burst of peppermint. Wakes the senses fast.

Chime and voice

A slow chime, then Ollie speaks to you.

Rising alarm

Fast beeps that grow louder. Never a sudden blast.

Always
A long look away from the road gets one short chime and doesn't climb the ladder. If your eyes stay shut for half a second, Ollie jumps straight to the alarm.
